Introduction

The Philippines is home to some of the most stunning natural wonders and historic landmarks in Southeast Asia. Here is one must-see spot from each of six destinations.

Baguio = Burnham Park

A large city park in the Summer Capital of the Philippines, with a man-made lake where visitors can rent boats and stroll through gardens.

Palawan = Puerto Princesa Underground River

A navigable underground river that flows directly into the sea, winding through a limestone cave filled with stalactites and stalagmites.

Cebu = Fort San Pedro

A Spanish-era military fortress in Cebu City, begun in 1565 and now a museum and park with stone walls and cannons.

Bohol = Chocolate Hills

More than a thousand symmetrical, cone-shaped hills spread across Bohol's countryside. They turn chocolate-brown in the dry season, giving the landmark its name.

Davao = Philippine Eagle Center

A conservation center dedicated to breeding and protecting the critically endangered Philippine eagle.

Siargao = Cloud 9

Known as the surfing capital of the Philippines, Siargao is famous for its Cloud 9 surf break and laid-back island atmosphere.
